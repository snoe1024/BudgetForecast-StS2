[b]Client-side mod: does not affect gameplay.[/b]

[b]Estimate how much Gold you'll have when you reach a shop.[/b]
Uses public information to [b]estimate[/b] your Gold on arrival at a shop. This is not a cheat mod.
Accounts for income and expenses from all relics, showing each party member's Gold and card removal cost side by side.
If your current room offers multiple choices, it shows the income and expenses for every option.
At the start of a run, see everyone's offers from Neow without having to ask.
Find out right away whether an early shop is worth visiting—or worth avoiding because several players are taking Silken Tress!

[b]Features[/b]
Hover over a reachable shop node ahead on the map to see:
[list]
[*] The route to the shop, based on your selected route strategy
[*] Every party member's minimum and average Gold (excluding event losses)
[*] Every party member's card removal cost (with relic discounts applied)
[*] Gold changes for any offered Ancient relics that affect Gold
[*] Choices and Gold changes during events that affect Gold
[/list]

While hovering over a shop node, you can also:
[list]
[*] Hold Shift for more details
[*] Use Shift + mouse wheel to switch route strategies (five in total)
[*] Use Shift + right-click to draw and share the displayed route
[/list]

[b]Branch support[/b]
Works on both the default and Public Beta branches.

[b]Requires[/b]
BaseLib (installed automatically as a Workshop dependency).

[b]Languages[/b]
English, Japanese, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese.
The developer is a Japanese speaker, so all other languages are machine-translated. Corrections are very welcome!
